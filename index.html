How to play

Setup. Every space starts filled, alternating black and white.

Opening. Black removes one stone from a corner or the center. White then removes a white stone directly next to that gap.

Jumps. On your turn, jump one of your stones over an adjacent enemy stone into the empty space just beyond it, orthogonally only — never diagonal. The jumped stone is captured.

Chains. You may keep jumping with the same stone as long as each jump is legal, but only in a straight line — no turning mid-move. Stopping early is always allowed.

Winning. If you cannot make a legal jump on your turn, you lose. There are no draws and no passes.
